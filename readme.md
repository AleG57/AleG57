## Resume

At the afertmath of a french preparatory class in engineering, I have been accepted in a general engineering programm at a french Grande école, CentraleSupélec, Paris-Saclay university, where I specialized in data science and AI for the last year. Meanwhile, I've worked for a subsidiary of EDF, the main electricity productor and provider in France as software engineer (one year) and data scientist (two years) in apprenticeship.

First of all, my work was related to continuous improvement of ICT systems of the company, including mobile, desktop apps development and SAS maintenance. The last two years of my apprenticeship was dedicated to data visualization, analysis and modeling of time series including anomaly detection and forecast.

I am currently enrolled in a PhD in deep learning at LORIA, CNRS, specialized in localized anomaly detection and uncertainty estimation in computer vision. On the same time, I am lab teacher in machine learning, structure and algorithms courses at CentraleSupélec.
